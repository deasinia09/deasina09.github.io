What Is Antisemitism?

Antisemitism is prejudice against or hatred of Jewish people. It can appear in stereotypes, conspiracy theories, discrimination, harassment, and violence.

Recognize

Learn how stereotypes, misinformation, and discrimination can contribute to antisemitism.

Educate

Understanding Jewish history and the history of antisemitism can help challenge misinformation.

Speak Up

When you encounter prejudice, supporting people who are targeted can help create a safer community.
